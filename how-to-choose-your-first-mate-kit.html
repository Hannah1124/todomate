First, understand mate

Mate is not just tea in a cup.

Yerba mate is traditionally prepared with dried leaves from the Ilex paraguariensis plant, placed inside a gourd and sipped through a filtered metal straw called a bombilla.

The drink is especially common across Argentina, Paraguay, Uruguay and southern Brazil, with roots often traced back to Indigenous Guaraní communities. Over time, mate became part of daily life, family gatherings and friendship across South America.

That is why mate feels different from ordinary tea or coffee. It is not only about flavour or energy. It is about a small ritual: preparing the gourd, placing the bombilla, adding hot water and taking a moment to slow down.

One gourd. One bombilla. A slower way to gather, talk and be present — whether you share it with others or enjoy it on your own.

The basic setup

What does a complete mate set include?

For beginners, the confusing part is usually not drinking mate — it is knowing which tools you actually need. A complete set brings the essentials together.

01

Yerba mate

The tea itself. Some blends are smoother and lighter; others are stronger and more intense.

02

Mate gourd

The cup used to prepare and drink mate. Stainless steel is easy to clean; wood or calabash feels more traditional.

03

Bombilla

The filtered metal straw that lets you drink mate without drinking the loose leaves.

04

Cleaning brush

A simple tool to help keep your bombilla clean, especially if you drink mate often.

05

Intro card

A short guide to help you prepare your first mate and care for your tools with confidence.
